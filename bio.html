MARLEON: THE ARTIST

Marleon is a conceptual artist and sculptor based in New York. Her work primarily explores the intersection of industrial materials and natural forms, challenging conventional notions of beauty and decay. Born in Rome in 2000, Marleon studied at the Rhode Island School of Design, where she first developed her signature style of incorporating salvaged metal and wood into monumental, yet delicate, installations.

Her most celebrated series, "Ephemeral Rust," has been exhibited internationally, from the Tate Modern to the Mori Art Museum. Marleone often uses processes of accelerated corrosion and meticulous layering, inviting viewers to reflect on permanence and the passage of time. She believes that the history embedded in discarded materials gives her creations an authentic, lived narrative.
